We're organizing events, fundraisers, and shelter support activities throughout the La Porte community. Check back for announcements and get involved when the opportunity arises.

What's Ahead

Building a Calendar of Community Support

Friends of La Porte Animal Shelter is in the process of organizing its first calendar of community events. Our planned activities include supply donation drives, community fundraisers, volunteer days, and public educational outreach — all centered on supporting the La Porte Animal Shelter and the animals in its care.

These events are for everyone in La Porte — whether you're a lifelong animal advocate, a first-time volunteer, or simply a neighbor who wants to give back. We'll have something meaningful for you to be part of.

Paint Your Pet

Support La Porte Animal Shelter

  • Time: 2:00 PM
  • Location: Painting with a Twist (3224 Spencer Hwy, Pasadena, TX)
  • Price: $60 per person

Join us for a fun, beginner-friendly Paint Your Pet event benefiting the Friends of La Porte Animal Shelter. Whether you’ve painted before or not, this guided class will walk you step-by-step through creating a custom painting of your pet.

What to Expect

  • Open to the public — invite friends and family
  • Most proceeds go directly to support the La Porte Animal Shelter
  • Bring your own wine, beer, or snacks
  • Relaxed, social environment with guided instruction

How It Works

  1. Reserve your spot
  2. Submit your pet’s photo as instructed
  3. Your image will be pre-sketched on your canvas before the event
  4. Show up and paint — everything else is handled!

Reserve your spot today — space is limited!

Every seat helps provide food, care, and medical support for animals.

Community Supply Drive

One of the most direct ways to help the shelter is to fill it with the supplies animals need. We'll organize a collection drive where La Porte residents can drop off food, bedding, toys, cleaning supplies, and other essential items for shelter use.

Whether you donate one can of food or a truckload of supplies, every contribution makes a real, immediate difference for the animals at the shelter. A full list of most-needed items will be posted when the event is confirmed.

Volunteer Day at the Shelter

Spend time directly at the La Porte Animal Shelter — meeting the animals, supporting shelter staff, and lending your hands to whatever is needed most. Whether you have one hour or a full day to give, your presence matters.

Why It Matters

Why Our Events Help the Shelter

Every event we host creates direct, tangible value for the shelter, the animals, and the community.

🐾
Better Animal Care

Supplies and funding from events go directly to improving the daily lives of shelter animals.

🤝
Stronger Community Bonds

Events build relationships between La Porte residents, creating lasting networks of support.

📣
Greater Awareness

Public events raise the shelter's visibility, bringing it new supporters and advocates.

💪
A Growing Volunteer Base

Events are one of the best ways to introduce new people to the shelter and inspire them to get involved.
