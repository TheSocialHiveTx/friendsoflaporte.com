Community Events

Events & Activities

We're organizing events, fundraisers, and shelter support activities throughout the La Porte community. Check back for announcements and get involved when the opportunity arises.

What's Ahead

Building a Calendar of Community Support

Friends of La Porte Animal Shelter is in the process of organizing its first calendar of community events. Our planned activities include supply donation drives, community fundraisers, volunteer days, and public educational outreach — all centered on supporting the La Porte Animal Shelter and the animals in its care.

These events are for everyone in La Porte — whether you're a lifelong animal advocate, a first-time volunteer, or simply a neighbor who wants to give back. We'll have something meaningful for you to be part of.

Why It Matters

Why Our Events Help the Shelter

Every event we host creates direct, tangible value for the shelter, the animals, and the community.

🐾
Better Animal Care

Supplies and funding from events go directly to improving the daily lives of shelter animals.

🤝
Stronger Community Bonds

Events build relationships between La Porte residents, creating lasting networks of support.

📣
Greater Awareness

Public events raise the shelter's visibility, bringing it new supporters and advocates.

💪
A Growing Volunteer Base

Events are one of the best ways to introduce new people to the shelter and inspire them to get involved.
