Eleanor has been a resident of La Porte for over 20 years and has always had a passion for animal welfare. She previously volunteered at multiple local rescues before leading the initiative to support the La Porte Animal Shelter. Her vision is to ensure every animal finds a loving home and that the shelter has the resources it needs to thrive.
David brings extensive experience in nonprofit management and community outreach. He joined the board to help streamline fundraising efforts and build stronger connections between the shelter and local businesses. When he's not working, David enjoys hiking with his two rescue dogs.
With a background in finance, Maria oversees the organization's budget and financial planning. She ensures that every dollar donated is maximized to directly benefit the animals. Maria is a proud cat mom to three foster-fails.
Robert is a retired educator who believes strongly in the power of community education. He leads our educational outreach programs, teaching local youth about responsible pet ownership and the importance of adopting from shelters.
Sarah is a professional event coordinator who donates her time to organize our community fundraisers and supply drives. Her energetic approach has significantly increased community participation in our events. She has been fostering puppies for the last five years.
Michael specializes in digital marketing and manages our online presence and community engagement campaigns. His efforts have helped expand our reach and connect more animals with potential adopters. He is a passionate advocate for adopting senior pets.
