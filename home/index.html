Who We Are

A Nonprofit Built Around Your Local Shelter

Friends of La Porte Animal Shelter was formed to be a dedicated community partner for the City of La Porte Animal Shelter. We believe every animal in our community deserves the support, care, and attention that only comes when neighbors come together.

Our role is to be a bridge — connecting caring residents, volunteers, and donors with the shelter that serves our community's animals every day of the year.

Our Focus Areas

Four Ways We Support the Shelter

Everything we do centers on these four pillars — bringing the La Porte community closer to the animals and people at the shelter.

🤝

Community Engagement

We work to connect La Porte residents, animal lovers, and local supporters with the shelter. From awareness campaigns to community events, we help people build a real relationship with their local shelter and see firsthand how they can make a difference.

💛

Fundraising

The shelter needs consistent resources to care for the animals in its charge. We raise funds to help cover essential needs like food, supplies, medicine, enrichment items, and other animal care resources — so the shelter can do more with more.

🙋

Volunteer Support

Volunteers are the lifeblood of any shelter community. We actively recruit, organize, and grow volunteer participation to ensure the shelter has hands-on support from people who care about the animals and the mission.

📚

Educational Outreach

Many people simply don't know what their local animal shelter does — or how they can help. We work to educate the public about shelter operations, responsible pet ownership, adoption awareness, and how community support directly improves animal welfare.
