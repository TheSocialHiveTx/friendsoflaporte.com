Why Join

Why Your Membership Matters

Friends of La Porte Animal Shelter is a community-powered nonprofit. Every member strengthens our ability to show up for the shelter, the animals, and the people of La Porte.

🏠

Strengthens Local Shelter Support

Members help give Friends of La Porte Animal Shelter the community foundation it needs to advocate for and support the shelter effectively. Every person who joins adds depth to what we can do.

📣

Expands Community Awareness

Members help spread the word. When you're part of this organization, you become an ambassador for the shelter's mission — helping more La Porte residents understand what the shelter does and how they can help.

💛

Supports Fundraising & Outreach

A larger membership base helps us raise more funds and run more effective outreach programs. Your involvement — even just by being a member — amplifies every effort we make.

🙋

Grows Volunteer & Donor Engagement

Members often become volunteers, donors, and advocates. Your involvement helps attract others — creating a ripple effect that brings more resources and energy to the shelter's mission.

Get Involved

What Member Involvement Looks Like

Membership is the starting point. Here's how members make a real difference in our community effort.

1

Support the Mission

Being a member means standing behind the values of this organization — community, care, and commitment to La Porte's animal shelter. Your belief in the mission is foundational.

2

Participate in Events

Members are invited to join our community events — supply drives, fundraisers, volunteer days, and more. These events are where the mission becomes action.

3

Help Spread Awareness

Share information about the shelter and our organization with your friends, neighbors, and social networks. Awareness is one of the most powerful tools we have.

4

Be Part of the Community Effort

This is a collective effort — neighbors supporting neighbors in the work of caring for animals. Members are the heartbeat of this organization.
